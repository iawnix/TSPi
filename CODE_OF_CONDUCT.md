# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Participants in the TSPi project must treat one another with respect. Useful
technical disagreement is welcome; harassment, discrimination, intimidation,
personal attacks, and unwanted contact are not.

Contributors should assume good intent, explain evidence and trade-offs, keep
reviews focused on the work, and protect private research data and credentials.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository maintainer through the
channel listed on the repository profile. Reports will be reviewed promptly and
kept confidential where possible. Maintainers may remove comments, reject
contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to keep the project
safe and constructive.

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and project
events.
