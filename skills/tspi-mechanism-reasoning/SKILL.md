# TSPi Mechanism Reasoning

[Chinese version](SKILL.zh-CN.md)

Use this Skill to define and challenge reaction-mechanism Claims, identify
elementary steps and competing pathways, or decide which observation would
distinguish alternatives. It reasons over verified Findings; it does not
replace transition-state validation, IRC, or energetics.

Define species, composition, charge, spin or electronic state, atom mapping,
bond changes, environment, and reversibility explicitly. Keep each material
mechanism statement in a Claim with predictions and falsifiers. Express support,
conflict, or dependency as relations between Claims. ResearchNode dependencies
describe study order, not chemical connectivity.

Separate species identity, elementary-step connectivity, saddle evidence,
energetics, kinetics, and robustness into questions that can fail independently.
Preserve competing mechanisms and unexpected results. When evidence challenges
the framing, revise or relate Claims and create a dependent Node for the new
question rather than rewriting prior work.
